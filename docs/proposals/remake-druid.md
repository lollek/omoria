## Shapeshifting example

Should it be part of druid a la 3.5E/Pathfinder, or Shifter class?
Should be possible to change into an animal form. 

- Should be available from the start of the game
- Should no longer be possible to use items.
- Equipment, except shield and armor, still applies
- Spell should probably still be castable
- Manacost? Makes non-shifting more viable if you don't have as many spells left.

**Shapeshift I** (Wolf):
- +2 strength
- +3 AC
- +2 AB
- 1d6 + 1 damage
- Low light vision, scent

**Shapeshift II** (Dire Wolf):
- +4 strength
- -2 dexterity
- +6 AC
- +7 AB
- 1d8 + 6 damage
- Low light vision, scent

**Shapeshift III** (Dire Bear):
- +4 strength
- -2 dexterity
- +11 AC
- +7 AB
- (1d6 + 7) x 2
- Low light vision, scent

**Shapeshift IV** (Worg):
- +4 strength
- +15 AC
- +7 AB
- (1d6 + 7) x 2, 1d8 + 7 damage
- Darkvision, scent
  
**Shapeshift V** (Aurumvorax):
- +6 strength
- +21 AC
- +11 AB
- 1d6 + 5, (1d4 + 5) x 4 damage
- 10 damage reduction
- Fireproof
- Darkvision, scent
